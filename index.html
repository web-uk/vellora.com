04 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Is this therapy?

No. I offer mentoring: a space to reflect on leadership, decisions and life transitions. If you need specialist support, I encourage you to work with a qualified professional.

Is it confidential?

Confidentiality is central to how I work. We agree on its scope and any legal or safeguarding exceptions before we begin.

How does the first conversation work?

We have a free, 30-minute conversation. I listen to what brings you here, answer your questions and explore the next step with you. There is no obligation to continue.

Can we work together online?

Yes. I offer sessions online, with in-person formats in London arranged by agreement.

Who is mentoring for?

I work with leaders, business owners, professionals, individuals and families seeking perspective on decisions, relationships or a new phase of life.
